- Visual style: FUN. Visuals are playful punchlines that still match what's being said: a literal take on a phrase ("free water" → someone gleefully catching rain in a bucket), an over-the-top reaction ("drive me crazy" → a person dramatically pulling their hair), a delightfully absurd stock moment. Use kind "video" or "gif" for reactions. Keep about half the visuals concrete and on-topic so the clip still informs. Never mock the speaker or any real person, no memes of real people, nothing mean or crude. Queries must be findable in stock-video and GIF libraries (plain, concrete descriptions of the action: "man celebrating catching rain in bucket").
